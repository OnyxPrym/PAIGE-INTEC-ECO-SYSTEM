About

Paige Intec Financial Suite

Version 5.0 · Single-file web app · Local + portable file storage

All data is stored on your device. Save regularly to your phone.

Quick Guide

1. Set up your company — Company tab. Every field is required.

2. Capture transactions with photos — Capture tab.

3. Save regularly — Save tab → "Save Full". Your phone offers WhatsApp, Drive, email, Files.

4. Payroll — add employees, generate payslips, post to books, IRP5 at year-end.

5. Reports — pick Monthly / Yearly / All-Time and generate any statement.

6. VAT — Category A/B/C periods build automatically.

Full Feature List

  • Company setup with hard lock
  • Rear + front camera with shutter, multi-photo per transaction
  • Ledger with filters, edit, undo delete
  • Save / Load to portable .json file
  • VAT A/B/C engine + VAT201 + Supporting Docs PDF
  • Financial reports — Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, Bank Recon, Debtors & Creditors, VAT Recon, Payroll Summary, Full Statements
  • Monthly / Yearly / All-Time periods tied to your FYE
  • Charts — Income/Expense breakdown + monthly net
  • Employees with medical aid, pension, UIF exemption
  • Payslip calculation (SARS 2025/26 PAYE, UIF, SDL, Medical credits)
  • Payslip PDF + post to books
  • IRP5 / PAYE Certificate at year-end
  • Audit log of every action
  • Quick-start wizard
  • Dashboard with at-a-glance stats
  • Trash with 30-second undo

Honest note: PAYE/UIF/SDL figures are computed from current SARS published rates. Verify with SARS or a registered payroll professional before any statutory submission. This app is a bookkeeping tool, not a substitute for professional accounting advice.
